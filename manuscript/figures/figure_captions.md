**Fig. 2.** Temporal sampling design and change metrics. (a) Sources by date: colonial correspondence and engineers' descriptions (1824-1884), Survey of India one-inch sheets (1922-24), CORONA frames (1965-68), and dry-season (January-March) Landsat and Sentinel-2 medians (1990-2026); dashed line: completion of the Malaprabha dam.
